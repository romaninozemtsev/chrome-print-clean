Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Print Clean browser extension ("Print Clean", "we", "us", or "our"), published by Monkey Skates LLC, handles your information. The short version: Print Clean does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

Print Clean does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not run analytics, we do not use tracking cookies, and we do not maintain any servers that receive your data.

2. Data the extension stores locally

To do its job, Print Clean saves your preferences: specifically the per-website rules describing which page elements to remove or keep when printing. This data is stored using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync API. It stays within your own browser and, if you are signed in to Chrome with sync enabled, is synchronized across your own devices by Google. We never receive a copy of it.

3. Page content

Print Clean reads and modifies the content of the page you are viewing only when you actively trigger it (by clicking the toolbar icon, using the keyboard shortcut, or choosing the right-click menu item). This processing happens entirely on your device. Page content is never sent to us or to any third party.

4. Permissions

The extension requests only the permissions it needs to function:

  • activeTab / scripting: to read and clean the current page when you invoke Print Clean.
  • storage: to save your per-website print rules.
  • contextMenus: to add the "Clean up and print" right-click option.

5. Third-party services

Print Clean does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, or data-broker services.

6. Children's privacy

Print Clean is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@monkeyskates.dev.
