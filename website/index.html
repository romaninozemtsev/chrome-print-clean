Chrome extension

Print web pages without the clutter.

Open an interactive print preview right on the page. Click anything to remove it, keep only what matters, then print exactly what you see. No more guess-and-reprint cycles, and no CSS knowledge required.

Add to Chrome — it's free See how it works

Why Print Clean

What you see is what prints

No surprise print stylesheets. Print Clean snapshots the curated, on-screen content so your printout matches the preview.

Click to remove

Hover highlights the target; click to drop it from the printout. Or "keep only" one section and everything else disappears.

Remembers your choices

Your rules are saved per website, so the next visit (and the quick right-click print) starts clean automatically.

Built-in site handlers

Sites like Gmail get special handling to keep just the email body, so common pages print perfectly out of the box.

Keyboard shortcut

Press ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + P to open the preview on any page, or right-click to print instantly.

Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no tracking, and none of your page content ever leaves your device.

How it works

1

Open the preview Click the toolbar icon (or press ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + P) to overlay an interactive preview on the live page.

2

Curate the page Click elements to remove them, or "keep only" a single section. Undo and reset are always one click away.

3

Print clean Hit Print and get a tidy printout that matches what you saw, with your choices remembered for next time.
